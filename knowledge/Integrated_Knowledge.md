# SANA OS - Integrated Knowledge Cheat Sheet

## 0. The SANA Principle & Zero-Doubt Observation

Historical irrationality and tragedies are not caused by moral defects or collective madness. They are rational adaptations and defensive reactions to the environmental pressures of their time.

* **Zero-Doubt Observation:** Instead of judging past actions as "wrong," observe as a biologist: "To which reward (survival) system were they trying to adapt?".
* **Structural Translation:** Translate the moral judgment "You are wrong" into "Given the premises of Layer A and Layer B at the time, this choice was a logical structural consequence".

## 1. Core Framework & The Continuum Theory

The foundational rules of separation and hierarchy for structural historical analysis.

* **Source Trifurcation:** Strictly separate the following:
* **Fact:** The event as it actually occurred.
* **Perception:** How contemporaries understood the event, which determines their actions.
* **Record:** The surviving evidence, which inherently includes editing and intent.


* **The Three Layers:**
* **a-layer (Survival):** Physical survival, resources, and safety.
* **b-layer (Culture):** Religion, values, and shared narratives (Narrative Core).
* **c-layer (Institution):** Laws, state apparatus, and formal institutions (Institution Core).


* **The Continuum Theory:** The b-layer (unwritten, liquid phase) and c-layer (written, solid phase) are the same "survival rules" in different states. When institutions (c) stabilize, they naturalize into culture (b). When culture (b) fails, it hardens into new institutions (c).

## 2. Diplomacy & Conflict Dynamics (Handshake & $\Delta v$)

Indicators of structural stability in interactions between communities.

* **Handshake Protocol:** The process of establishing a non-hostile baseline.
* **SYN:** Signaling cooperation and premise alignment (e.g., sending envoys).
* **ACK:** Acceptance of the signal and structural stabilization.


* **Handshake Failure:** If an ACK is refused or forced under humiliating conditions, structural friction ($\Delta v$) breaks its limits, cascading into physical a-layer conflict (war).

## 3. Structural Load & Collapse (Time / RBM / Legitimacy)

Macro-level variables explaining why states and systems collapse.

* **Time Layer:**
* A civilization's absorption capacity depends on the slower of its narrative or institutions: $Absorption\ Capacity = \min(V_B, V_C)$.
* When environmental velocity ($V_A$) exceeds this capacity, a Time Lag crisis occurs: $\Delta T = V_A - \min(V_B, V_C)$, leading to systemic failure.


* **RBM (Resource Overload):**
* Prolonged structural overload where demand exceeds supply for critical functions ($Gap_j = Demand_j - Supply_j$) renders the system unsustainable.


* **Legitimacy Layer:**
* When the divergence between laws (Institution Core) and people's identity (Narrative Core) exceeds a critical threshold, the structural adhesive is lost, leading to revolution or civil war (Void formation).



## 4. Micro-Gravity & Human Choice (Proximity Rule)

Explains why individuals prioritize local commands over state laws.

* **Proximity Rule:** Humans calculate the priority of a rule based on its distance from their survival: $Priority = Impact / Distance$.
* **Trust Dynamics:** Trust is a feedback loop of protection (rewards). When a state fails to provide protection, individuals migrate to new communities to secure survival.
* **Gravity Inversion:** Prioritizing immediate punishment/reward from a local commander (Local b-layer) over the delayed punishment of a distant state law (Global c-layer) is a biologically rational "Priority Filtering", not moral corruption.

## 5. Cognitive Compression & Role Failure (CPM / RSM)

Fatal bugs in human psychology and organizational structure during macro-crises.

* **Cognitive Compression (CPM):** Under high survival pressure and uncertainty, humans lose the capacity to explore. To rapidly eliminate anxiety, defense mechanisms trigger rapid narrative substitution (e.g., witch hunts) and enemy construction.
* **Role System Failure (RSM):**
* **Role Capture:** Operational units (Executor, e.g., the military) forcibly override and absorb the authority of the decision-makers (Decider).
* **Architect Isolation:** The system designer (reformer) fails to translate structural changes into the shared cultural narrative (b-layer), losing legitimacy and being expelled.
